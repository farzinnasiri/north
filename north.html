DESIGN SPEC · V1

North

A starting point for men who've lost the thread. One action a day, a coach's voice, and a clear direction. Built so that one day you don't need it.

Colour

Deep field is the base — near-black with a blue cast, closer to pre-dawn sky than to gym black. Brass carries every action. Meridian marks what's steady and earned. Ember is held in reserve.

Brass

#C9A227 Primary accent. Today's action, the day count, the one button that matters. Never more than one brass element in view at a time.

Meridian

#1F4E8C Steady state. Completed actions, held streaks, anything already earned. Light variant #5C8FD6 for text on dark.

Ember

#A32D2D Held back. A broken streak, a hard truth, a moment that needs weight. If it appears weekly it has lost its force.
#F2F0EB paper · #9BA3AD ash · #6B6E73 slate · #2A3038 line · #1A1F27 raise · #12161C field · #0F1319 field deep

Paper is #F2F0EB, not #FFFFFF. The warmth reads as paper rather than screen, and it keeps the whole interface from feeling clinical.

Type

Inter for everything spoken. JetBrains Mono for everything counted. The split is the point: prose is human, numbers are facts.

Principles

Rules for anyone building on this. When a decision is unclear, these break the tie.

01

One thing per screen

He opens it and sees today's action. Not a dashboard, not eight metrics. Every choice you hand him is a chance to close the app.

02

Earn the accent

Brass means "this is the thing." One brass element in view. The moment it's on three things it stops meaning anything.

03

Never shout

No caps, no exclamation marks, no CRUSH IT. Strength doesn't shout or posture — the interface holds the same line.

04

No trophies

No confetti, badges, or levels. He's building a self, not a collection. Celebration undercuts the seriousness of the thing.

05

Numbers are facts

Mono face, no decoration. Progress is measured and stated plainly, the way a log is kept, not the way a game is scored.

06

Missing is not failing

A broken streak gets one honest sentence and an open door. Guilt is the thing that made him stop opening apps like this.

07

Coach, not guru

Direct, warm, unsentimental. Says the hard thing once and moves on. Never mystical, never a hype man, never his friend.

08

Build the exit

Success is deletion. Milestones point toward not needing it. This is the promise no competitor is making — keep it visible.

The code

Not shown on first open. It's offered once, on day one, and lives one tap from the daily screen after that. Read, not pushed.

THE CODE

For any man starting over

A call to reclaim strength, courage, and character in an age of noise.

We don't cry for sympathy. We don't beg for ease.

Pain is part of the process, and we don't run from it. We carry it, crush it, learn from it.

We don't whine. We don't complain to gain applause.

We share our scars as lessons, not as excuses. We bleed quietly, rebuild silently, and rise without asking permission.

We solve problems. We own our mistakes. We act decisively.

When the world presses down, we press back harder. Not with noise, but with resolve.

Our strength doesn't shout or posture.

It's in patience, purpose, and presence. In quiet acts of bravery, and in the consistency of character when nobody else is watching.

This is not about ego or perfection, nor about suppressing emotion. It's about honouring ourselves, standing firm when life bends us, and refusing to become victims of circumstance.

It's about becoming the kind of man others can lean on — not the kind who always needs a shoulder.

We choose responsibility over blame, discipline over indulgence, and dignity over drama.

We are men — not by accident, but by action. Not defined by bravado, but by backbone.

Strength. Stillness. Sovereignty.
This is the code.

Voice

Somewhere between Mark Manson and David Goggins. Honest about how hard it is, unwilling to let him off the hook, never cruel.
