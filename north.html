DESIGN SPEC · V1

North

A starting point for men who've lost the thread. One action a day, a coach's voice, and a clear direction. Built so that one day you don't need it.

Colour

Warm mineral paper is the base. Ink carries the voice and the action. Brass is reduced to one measured edge, index, or needle. Meridian marks what is steady and earned. Ember is held in reserve.

Brass

#7D5525 One precise material detail: needle, index, trace, or edge. Never a large button fill and never more than one brass element in view.

Meridian

#31545B Steady state. Completed actions, held progress, anything already earned. The oxidised tone holds its weight on mineral paper.

Ember

#8A3E34 Held back. A broken streak, a hard truth, a moment that needs weight. If it appears weekly it has lost its force.
#181814 ink · #54534C ash · #69675F slate · #B8B0A1 line · #DCD5C8 raise · #E9E4DA sheet · #F2EFE7 field

The field is #F2EFE7, not #FFFFFF. The mineral warmth reads as a physical page rather than a blank screen, while ink stays softer than pure black.

Type

Newsreader gives statements and questions editorial presence. Inter explains without performance. JetBrains Mono is reserved for facts that are actually counted.

Principles

Rules for anyone building on this. When a decision is unclear, these break the tie.

01

One thing per screen

He opens it and sees today's action. Not a dashboard, not eight metrics. Every choice you hand him is a chance to close the app.

02

Earn the accent

Brass means "this is the thing." One brass element in view. The moment it's on three things it stops meaning anything.

03

Never shout

No caps, no exclamation marks, no CRUSH IT. Strength doesn't shout or posture — the interface holds the same line.

04

No trophies

No confetti, badges, or levels. He's building a self, not a collection. Celebration undercuts the seriousness of the thing.

05

Numbers are facts

Mono face, no decoration. Progress is measured and stated plainly, the way a log is kept, not the way a game is scored.

06

Missing is not failing

A broken streak gets one honest sentence and an open door. Guilt is the thing that made him stop opening apps like this.

07

Coach, not guru

Direct, warm, unsentimental. Says the hard thing once and moves on. Never mystical, never a hype man, never his friend.

08

Build the exit

Success is deletion. Milestones point toward not needing it. This is the promise no competitor is making — keep it visible.

The code

Outside onboarding and available from You. It is optional, readable, and never pushed.

THE CODE

The Code

North asks for your word, not a performance.

01

Tell yourself the truth, especially when it is inconvenient.

02

Choose what you will do. Name it clearly. Give it a deadline you can defend.

03

Keep your word when you can. When you miss, admit it without punishment or excuse. Then choose again.

04

Ask for help when you need it. Strength includes knowing what you cannot carry alone.

05

Treat other people as people, not tests of your worth.

06

Leave North when you can hold your direction without it.

Voice

Somewhere between Mark Manson and David Goggins. Honest about how hard it is, unwilling to let him off the hook, never cruel.

WriteNot
Do the thing you've been avoiding.Time to crush your goals! 💪
You missed three days. That's what happened.Oops! You broke your streak.
It costs ten minutes.Just take a few simple minutes!
Most men don't come back. You did.You're crushing it, king.
You don't need this anymore.Upgrade to keep your progress.

Never "just", "simply", or "easy" — they presume, and they condescend to a man who is finding it genuinely hard. Never call him king, brother, warrior, or legend.
