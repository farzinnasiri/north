PRODUCT SPEC · V1 · DECISIONS + OPEN PROBLEMS

North

One action at a time, held to a deadline you set yourself, until you don't need it anymore. Seven months, if you keep your word.

02

Product principles

These break ties. When a feature argument can't be settled, the principle wins over the feature.

Every touch is precious

Users switch apps constantly and arrive with no focus. Do few things, do them better than anyone, and never spend a screen on something that isn't the point.

Simplicity is complicated

The simple surface is the expensive part. Complexity gets absorbed by the system, never handed to the user.

No notifications, no reminders

The app never chases him. Responsibility sits with the user by design — he signed for it. This is the core differentiator and it is not negotiable.

Not a generic app giving life advice

Heavy personalisation throughout. A face that reacts. Content shaped by his onboarding. He should never feel he's reading a template.

Flexible, not one template of a man

Eight ordinary-life candidates reflect different priorities and costs. The person chooses a direction; the app does not diagnose his personality or convert him to one ideal.

Challenge the behaviour, never the man

State the observed choice or missed action plainly. Do not use unsupported population claims or goad him with invented odds.

Built to be outgrown

Success is deletion. The exit is stated up front, designed into the UI, and honoured when reached. No retention tricks past that line.

Apple-award UI or don't bother

The design is a reason to open it. Creative, fresh, considered. In a category of red-and-black hype apps, restraint is the differentiator.

03

Onboarding — the two pillars before the loop

Long, but creative. It earns its length by producing the two things the whole app runs on: where he actually stands, and what he's walking toward.

Pillar one — the reality

Capture recent conduct across six Axes. Reality provides the current side of the starting Direction; the chosen North Star provides the destination side.

  • Produces the initial direction, 0–180°
  • Feeds the state machine's picture of his weak areas
  • Re-run on recalibration after long absence

Pillar two — the north star

Not a 1-month, 1-year or 10-year goal. It is an authored life direction, framed through ordinary priorities and tradeoffs.

  • Two preference questions order eight authored archetypes
  • The Patriarch, Modern Man, Rogue, Laughing Wolf, Godfather, Kind Father, Freethinker, and Playboy
  • All archetypes begin unselected; the person must choose explicitly

The closing letter. Onboarding ends with a short letter that states plainly that he will outgrow North and that a miss never moves him backward. The optional Code lives outside onboarding in You.

04

The action loop

There is no daily reset. One action is live until it's closed. The next arrives when he starts it, not when the calendar turns.

StepRule
System proposesThe action comes from the system. Abstract, not concrete — "get out of your comfort zone", "message someone you haven't spoken to in a while". Never a specific instruction that could be acted on literally and harmfully.
He names itBefore starting, he writes his own version of it. The abstraction is deliberate: deciding what it means today is itself the muscle being trained.
He sets the deadlineMinimum 1 day, maximum 7 days. He chooses.
A lesson attachesShort text piece tied to that action. This is where the domain material lives.
He closes it outPure honour system. No verification, ever. The app cannot fix a man who won't be honest with himself, and pretending otherwise would insult the ones who are.
Optional noteA reflection field, never required, never used as proof. This is not a journaling app.
Swaps3 per month. For actions that genuinely don't fit his circumstances. Costly enough that "I don't feel like it" isn't worth spending one.
05

The direction

Degrees off north. Starts at up to 180° after onboarding. Not a streak — it cannot be broken, only moved or left still.

RuleValue
Range0°–180°. Onboarding sets the starting point.
Starting DirectionThe weighted gap across all six Reality Axes. Every Axis has weight; the chosen North Star increases emphasis on the gaps most relevant to that destination.
Base rewardClosing an action on a 1-day deadline earns 1.0°.
Duration cost−0.05° per day of deadline length. A 7-day action earns 0.65°. Pace shapes the curve; it is not punitive.
Missed deadlineEarns 0°. The needle stays still. Nothing is taken away.
Abandoned actionNo decay. The direction never moves backwards through inactivity.
Accuracy bonusClosing on the day he said earns the highest score. Rewards self-knowledge, not speed.
RecalibrationAbsence of ~1 month triggers a partial re-run of onboarding. The direction is remeasured, not punished — a man who vanished for a month genuinely is further out.
Nudge, not decayDeadline blown and Action still open: a factual message on Now. "You said three days. That was eleven days ago."
The exitReach 0° and hold 30 days. Best case: 180 days + 30 = 7 months. This is the pitch.

Do not build the naive early-finish bonus. If setting a 7-day deadline and finishing on day one pays more than an honest 1-day commitment, the optimal strategy becomes sandbagging — the exact opposite of the character being built. Any bonus must cap below what an accurate estimate would have earned.

Watch the slow-pace curve. At 0.65° per action, a man working consistently at 7-day pace needs ~277 actions and roughly 5 years. The 7-month promise is best case only. Decide before launch whether the pitch is honest for a slower man, or whether the duration cost needs flattening further.

06

Content engine

No LLM, no AI in v1. Fixed sequence plus a deterministic state machine over his onboarding data and history.

  • Fixed sequence — authored by hand, quality and voice guaranteed, no per-user cost
  • State machine — selects from the sequence based on his profile, weak areas, and what he's been avoiding
  • Deterministic — same inputs, same output. Debuggable and testable
  • Swappable later — once real data shows which prompts land, generation can be revisited

Domain logic and content come from the founder's existing notes and material. Structuring that corpus into the sequence is a known, deferred task.

07

Open problems

Unresolved. Roughly in the order they block progress.

The axes — biggest blocker

What four to six dimensions of a man's life does North track? This determines what onboarding asks, what the direction averages, and how the system detects avoidance. Body / work / money / social / romantic / inner is the obvious cut and probably too generic. Nothing downstream can be finalised until this is settled.

How the starting direction is calculated

Onboarding produces a number between 0 and 180. The mapping from his answers to that number is undefined, and it sets the length of his entire journey.

Two touches or one

Naming happens at the start, closing at the end. On a 1-day deadline that's two sessions in a day. Confirm this is the intended cost given the "every touch is precious" principle.

Accuracy bonus mechanics

Rewarding an accurate estimate over raw speed is decided in principle. The actual formula, and its interaction with the duration cost, is not.

What happens after the exit

He reaches north and North closes. Is there an archive, a quiet mode, an alumni state, a door back in if he slips? Currently just two buttons and no logic behind them.

Safety floor

The target user is described as lonely and depressed. Abstract prompts reduce liability but not risk. Needs a decision on crisis signals, what the app does when it sees one, and where the line sits between a coach and a clinician.

Business model

Untouched. Complicated by design: an app built to be outgrown in 7 months cannot rely on long-term subscription retention.

YouTube channel tie-in

Planned as the channel's companion app. No decisions yet on what that integration is, or whether it changes onboarding and acquisition.

Where the reverse psychology sits

Agreed in principle for onboarding and "in different forms" later. Which moments, and the taper rule as he progresses, are undefined.
