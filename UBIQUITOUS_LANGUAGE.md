# Ubiquitous language

Accepted vocabulary for North. Open terms are deliberately excluded until the
product specification settles them.

| Term | Definition | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person | The person using North. Use more specific product nouns when possible. | Player, follower |
| Reality | The onboarding picture of where a person currently stands. | Scorecard, diagnosis |
| North Star | The kind of person the person chooses to move toward. | Goal, ideal man |
| Bearing | Degrees from the North Star, from 180 degrees to 0 degrees. | Score, level, streak |
| Action | One system-proposed direction that the person interprets, names, and commits to. | Task, challenge, mission |
| Named Action | The person's concrete interpretation of an Action. | Proof, assignment |
| Open Action | The single Action currently in progress until closed or let go. | Daily task, today's action |
| Deadline | The one-to-seven-day commitment chosen for an Open Action. | Due date set by North |
| Closeout | The honor-system act of completing an Open Action. | Verification, submission |
| Note | An optional reflection attached to an Action. | Journal entry, evidence |
| Swap | One of three monthly choices to replace an unsuitable proposed Action. | Skip, reroll |
| Lesson | A short authored text attached to an Action. | AI advice, content card |
| Recalibration | A partial return to Reality after a long absence. | Penalty, reset |
| Hold North | The 30-day period after reaching a Bearing of 0 degrees. | Final streak |
| Exit | The designed point at which North is no longer needed. | Churn, cancellation |

Relationships:

- Onboarding establishes a Reality, a North Star, an initial Bearing, and a
  signed contract.
- At most one Open Action exists at a time.
- An Action becomes open only after the person names it and chooses a Deadline.
- A Closeout may include a Note, but neither requires proof.
- A successful Closeout may move the Bearing; a missed Deadline never moves it
  backward.
- Reaching 0 degrees begins Hold North. Completing Hold North leads to Exit.
